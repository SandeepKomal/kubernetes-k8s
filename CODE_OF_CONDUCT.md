# Code of conduct

This is a place to learn, and everyone here was a beginner once.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people asking basic questions.
- Explain *why*, not just *what*. Link to the level or doc that helps.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you are unsure.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Mocking someone for not knowing something.
- Sharing someone's private information.
- Posting credentials, kubeconfigs or other secrets (yours or anyone's).

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [@SandeepKomal](https://github.com/SandeepKomal), privately through
GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and block people who break this code of conduct.

This code follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
