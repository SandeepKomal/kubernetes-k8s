`kubectl describe pvc ledger-data` and `kubectl get storageclass`. Which
classes exist on this cluster?
---
You cannot change a claim's storageClassName. Delete the claim (and the Pod
using it) and create them again without `fast-ssd`.
---
kubectl delete pod ledger
kubectl delete pvc ledger-data
Remove the `storageClassName: fast-ssd` line from a copy of the level's
setup.yaml, then `kubectl apply -f` it.
