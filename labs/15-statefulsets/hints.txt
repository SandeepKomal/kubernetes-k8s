Compare `kubectl get svc orders-db` with what a StatefulSet needs for
per-Pod DNS names. What does the CLUSTER-IP column show?
---
The Service must have `clusterIP: None`, which cannot be edited in place:
export it, add `clusterIP: None`, delete it and create it again. Then scale
the StatefulSet to 3.
---
kubectl get svc orders-db -o yaml > svc.yaml
(remove clusterIP, clusterIPs, resourceVersion, uid; add clusterIP: None under spec)
kubectl delete svc orders-db && kubectl apply -f svc.yaml
kubectl scale statefulset orders-db --replicas=3
