Compare `kubectl describe service menu-api` (look at Selector) with
`kubectl get pods --show-labels`. Do they agree?
---
The selector says `app=menu`, the Pods say `app=menu-api`. Fixing only that
would also match the debug Pod. Which label is different on the debug Pod?
---
Select on both labels:
  kubectl patch service menu-api --type=merge -p '{"spec":{"selector":{"app":"menu-api","tier":"web"}}}'
Or `kubectl edit service menu-api` and change the selector by hand.
