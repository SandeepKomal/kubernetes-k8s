`kubectl logs deploy/web -c fetch-menu` shows the init container's error.
Compare the path it writes to with where the volume is mounted.
---
The command writes to /wrok instead of /work. After fixing it, add a
second entry under initContainers with `restartPolicy: Always`, the same
`html` volume mounted at /work, and a loop that writes `date` to
/work/updated.txt every 30 seconds.
---
See solution.sh for the full patch. The sidecar is:
  - name: menu-refresher
    image: busybox:1.36
    restartPolicy: Always
    command: ["sh", "-c", "while true; do date > /work/updated.txt; sleep 30; done"]
    volumeMounts: [{name: html, mountPath: /work}]
