`kubectl get networkpolicy` and `kubectl describe networkpolicy allow-everything`.
An ingress rule of `{}` means "from anywhere".
---
You need two policies: one that selects every Pod and allows nothing, and
one that selects `app=db` and allows ingress from Pods with `app=api` on
TCP port 80. Then delete allow-everything.
---
See the YAML in the Field notes for the default deny. The db policy:
  spec:
    podSelector: {matchLabels: {app: db}}
    policyTypes: ["Ingress"]
    ingress:
      - from: [{podSelector: {matchLabels: {app: api}}}]
        ports: [{protocol: TCP, port: 80}]
