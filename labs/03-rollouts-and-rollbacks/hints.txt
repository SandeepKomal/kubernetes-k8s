Start with `kubectl rollout history deployment/storefront` and
`kubectl get pods`. Which revision worked?
---
`kubectl rollout undo` goes back to the previous revision. Scaling is
`kubectl scale`. The strategy lives under `spec.strategy.rollingUpdate`.
---
kubectl rollout undo deployment/storefront
kubectl scale deployment/storefront --replicas=3
kubectl patch deployment storefront --type=merge -p '{"spec":{"strategy":{"rollingUpdate":{"maxUnavailable":0,"maxSurge":1}}}}'
kubectl rollout status deployment/storefront
