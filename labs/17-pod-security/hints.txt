`kubectl describe rs -l app=payments` lists every rule the Pod breaks.
---
You need a pod-level securityContext (runAsNonRoot, a non-zero runAsUser
such as 65534, seccompProfile RuntimeDefault) and a container-level one
(allowPrivilegeEscalation false, capabilities drop ALL,
readOnlyRootFilesystem true).
---
kubectl patch deployment payments --type=strategic -p '{"spec":{"template":{"spec":{
  "securityContext":{"runAsNonRoot":true,"runAsUser":65534,"seccompProfile":{"type":"RuntimeDefault"}},
  "containers":[{"name":"payments","securityContext":{"allowPrivilegeEscalation":false,
  "readOnlyRootFilesystem":true,"capabilities":{"drop":["ALL"]}}}]}}}}'
