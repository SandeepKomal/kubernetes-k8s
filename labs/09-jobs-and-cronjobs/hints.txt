`kubectl get cronjob cleanup -o yaml` shows every field from the mission.
`kubectl explain cronjob.spec` describes them.
---
One `kubectl patch cronjob cleanup --type=merge -p '{"spec":{...}}'` can set
all four fields. Then look at `kubectl create job --help` for `--from`.
---
kubectl patch cronjob cleanup --type=merge -p '{"spec":{"suspend":false,"schedule":"*/15 * * * *","concurrencyPolicy":"Forbid","successfulJobsHistoryLimit":3}}'
kubectl create job cleanup-now --from=cronjob/cleanup
