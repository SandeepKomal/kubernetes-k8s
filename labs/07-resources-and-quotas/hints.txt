`kubectl get deploy reports`, then `kubectl describe rs -l app=reports` and
`kubectl describe quota`. Is the quota or the scheduler saying no?
---
64 CPUs is more than the quota allows and more than any kind node has.
`kubectl set resources` changes requests and limits in one command.
---
kubectl set resources deployment reports --requests=cpu=100m,memory=64Mi --limits=cpu=100m,memory=64Mi
kubectl rollout status deployment/reports
