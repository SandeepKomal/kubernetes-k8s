There are four faults: two stop the Pods starting, one stops them becoming
Ready, and one stops traffic reaching them. Start with
`kubectl describe pod -l app=storefront` and read every Event.
---
Look closely at: the image tag, the name of the ConfigMap the volume
mounts versus `kubectl get configmaps`, the readiness probe's port, and the
Service's selector versus the Pods' labels.
---
kubectl set image deployment/storefront web=nginx:1.27-alpine
kubectl patch deployment storefront --type=json -p '[
  {"op":"replace","path":"/spec/template/spec/volumes/0/configMap/name","value":"storefront-page"},
  {"op":"replace","path":"/spec/template/spec/containers/0/readinessProbe/httpGet/port","value":80}]'
kubectl patch service storefront --type=merge -p '{"spec":{"selector":{"app":"storefront"}}}'
